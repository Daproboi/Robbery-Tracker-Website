How Pity Works

Progressive System

Each pity level increases your Hyperchrome drop chance by 1%, starting at 1% and reaching 50% at level 50.

Server Differences

Big servers reset pity every 24 hours, while small servers maintain pity across server restarts.

How to Calculate

Enter your total robberies and Hyperchromes earned. The calculator will determine your current pity level and progress.
